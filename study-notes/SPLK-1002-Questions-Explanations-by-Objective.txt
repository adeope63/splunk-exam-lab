OBJECTIVE 1.1: Use the chart command

YT-SPLK-032 [direct]
Which transforming commands share statistical aggregation functions?

A. stats, chart, timechart ✅ CORRECT ANSWER
   Correct. stats, chart, and timechart use statistical aggregation functions such as count, sum, and avg. Their result layouts differ.
B. inputlookup, lookup, outputlookup
   Incorrect. Lookup commands read or apply lookup data; they do not provide the stats/chart/timechart aggregation family.
C. search, where, transaction
   Incorrect. These commands have different expression/correlation purposes and do not share the same transforming aggregation function set.
D. table, fields, dedup
   Incorrect. table and fields project columns; dedup removes duplicate results rather than providing that aggregation function family.

Explanation: stats, chart, and timechart use statistical aggregation functions such as count, sum, and avg. Their result layouts differ.
